## Three tracks.One day to build.

## Pick a direction.Make it yours.

Choose a starting point first. Shared workshop resources will help you move from idea to demo.

Track 01 / Healthcare

### Track 1: Caregivers Challenge

Build multi-agent healthcare personas — Lab Analyst, Radiologist, Doctor, Nutritionist & Physio — that analyze lab results, radiology images, and EKG timeseries to deliver patient-friendly diagnoses, prescriptions, nutrition plans, and exercise guidance. Uses synthetic/public data only (no HIPAA compliance required).

[View track background](https://atx.rahman.dev/track-1.html)

Track 02 / Industrial AI

### Track 2: Autonomic Shop Floor & Predictive Maintenance

Create a multi-agent system that ingests temperature, energy, vibration, and lube monitor data to predict equipment failure, estimate useful life, auto-order parts, and schedule maintenance. Leverage open-source datasets (AI4I, CWRU Bearing Data) and machine manuals.

[View track background](https://atx.rahman.dev/track-2.html)

Track 03 / Finance

### Track 3: Financial Portfolio Management

Build agents that analyze historical price patterns, company financials, macroeconomic indicators, market sentiment, and geopolitical factors to construct investment portfolios. Use open-source SEC data and past market data — portfolios will be evaluated against actual performance.

[View track background](https://atx.rahman.dev/track-3.html)
