AWS experience and general cloud knowledge is expected from participants

This open-ended workshop that will allow participants to build and explore the latest advancements in Generative AI-powered content creation, applications, conversational assistants, and more. They will leverage AWS comprehensive suite of generative AI, ML, storage, database and serverless services to develop innovative solutions across a diverse range of domains.

The ideal participants for this workshop are those who have a firm grasp of AWS services and possess fundamental programming proficiencies. The target personas are:

- Solution Architects
- Software Development Engineers
- Generative AI Enthusiasts

Whether you're an experienced cloud architect, a talented software engineer, or simply captivated by the possibilities of generative AI, this workshop is designed for participants who will collaborate in teams to conceptualize, build, and showcase their creations. They will receive guidance and support from Solution Architects from AWS as well as from AWS champions within their own organization.
